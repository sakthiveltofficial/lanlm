# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want LanLM to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps build a positive community:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, especially to newcomers
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Assuming good intent, and asking when something is unclear
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people who use it
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling or discriminatory jokes
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Publishing other people's private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions or pull requests
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take fair, appropriate action in response to behaviour they find unacceptable. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers by using GitHub's **"Report content"** option, or by contacting the repository owners through the contact details on their GitHub profiles. All reports are handled promptly and confidentially. Maintainers will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow these steps, adjusting for severity: a private note asking for the behaviour to stop; a public or private warning; a temporary ban from the project spaces; a permanent ban.
